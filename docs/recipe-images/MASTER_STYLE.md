# PLANAM Gold V3 Master Style

## Style profile

`planam_gold_v3_master` is a modern homemade food photography style for PLANAM. The image should look like a real meal prepared at home in one consistent bright kitchen, not a stock image or restaurant campaign.

## Master prompt

Create a realistic editorial food photograph of the specified home-cooked dish. Make the dish the clear subject, served in a modern light neutral ceramic vessel appropriate to the meal. Use a bright home kitchen with a light countertop or light wood/light stone surface, soft natural window daylight, gentle warm clean shadows, and a believable homemade finish. Use a 3/4 or slight top-down angle, natural proportions, appetizing texture, restrained garnish, and enough negative space for responsive crops. Keep the visual world consistent across the PLANAM recipe library.

## Negative prompt

No text, watermark, logo, packaging, branded boxes, people, hands, clutter, messy table, plastic look, stock-photo look, restaurant fine-dining plating, dramatic studio lighting, random plates, bright patterns, branded items, collage, or extra unrelated objects.

## Vessel guidance

- Soup, porridge, and ragout: deep light bowl.
- Main dish: simple flat light plate.
- Salad: shallow neutral bowl or plate.
- Breakfast: small light plate or bowl.

## Image discipline

Recommended master format is 1536x1024, 3:2. Derived outputs are `hero.webp` 1200x675, `card_800.webp` 800x800, and `thumb_400.webp` 400x400. Keep the dish centered enough for all crops. A bad crop goes to manual review; do not generate a separate master for a variant.
