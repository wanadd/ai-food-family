# 00 Project Identity

## What PLANAM is

PLANAM is a personal and family nutrition assistant delivered as a Telegram Mini App. It turns household context into a ready food decision: what to cook, what to buy, what is at home, and what health constraints matter.

The core outcome is less daily decision fatigue. Recipes, nutrition facts, shopping, pantry state, family profiles, and AI are means to that outcome.

## What PLANAM is not

PLANAM is not a recipe catalog, calorie tracker, shopping-list utility, or open-ended chat product. It can contain those capabilities, but the product is judged by whether it prepares a useful next action.

## Audience

The primary audience is a person responsible for household food decisions. A household can operate in personal mode or optionally include family members, including virtual children who do not have Telegram accounts.

## Product promise

One place for a household to decide, prepare, buy, and understand food. The interface should feel calm, practical, warm, and trustworthy rather than clinical, gamified, or operationally dense.
