# 01 Product Principles

1. **Ready decision over information volume.** Every surface should help answer what to do next.
2. **AI in the background.** Users receive a prepared result and can ask for explanation or adjustment when useful.
3. **Health is contextual.** Health guidance is attached to a person, food, portion, process state, and evidence quality.
4. **Family is shared where it helps.** Menu and shopping are household-level, while restrictions, preferences, and safety are person-level.
5. **Unknown remains explicit.** Missing, ambiguous, or low-confidence evidence never silently becomes safe, zero, or unrestricted.
6. **Safety outranks convenience.** The family decision order is `BLOCK > ESCALATE > UNKNOWN > WARN > SAFE`.
7. **Evidence has authority.** Source, provenance, version, transformation, and confidence must be inspectable.
8. **One coherent product.** Menu, shopping, pantry, health, and family are views of the same household state.
9. **Progressive disclosure.** The first screen is light; detail is available when a user needs to verify or decide.
10. **Prepared for delivery, not coupled to it.** Shopping can later connect to delivery providers through an adapter boundary.

## Product language

Use concrete actions such as `Приготовить`, `Купить`, `Проверить`, and `Что важно`. Avoid presenting the product as a technical data system or as a medical authority.
